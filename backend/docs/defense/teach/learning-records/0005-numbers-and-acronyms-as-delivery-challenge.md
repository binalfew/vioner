# Numbers and acronyms are their own delivery skill

Slide 3 introduces a new category of delivery challenge: density of *named objects* — five numbers (30k, 20+, 54, ~30, 2024) plus six institutional acronyms (AU-PSC, AU-CEWS, ECOWAS, IGAD, UN OCHA, UNHCR) in a 75-second slot. None of the earlier lessons exercised this. Stumbling on a number or scrambling an acronym is a recognisable tell of unfamiliarity, regardless of how strong the underlying knowledge is.

## Implications

- Future slides with dense numerical or institutional content (slides 4, 6, 17) should reuse the chunking and acronym-pronunciation techniques established here.
- The "not an academic curiosity" strategic move belongs to a small set of mission-relevance assertions the user needs to land naturally; track when this same beat appears elsewhere.
- The user's prior work (concepts_explained, formulas_explained) has prepared them on the *substance* of the numbers, not on saying them aloud under pressure. This is the gap.
