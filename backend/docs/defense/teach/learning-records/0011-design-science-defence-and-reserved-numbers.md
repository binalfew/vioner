# Design science as defendable methodology + the reserved-numbers technique

Slide 9 introduces two new craft skills with deep transferability across the rest of the deck. First, *design science as a defendable methodology* — converting "I built and tested" (which sounds like engineering) into "I iterated on a research artefact with measured signals closing each loop" (which is methodologically distinct). Second, the *reserved-numbers technique* — withholding specific data (κ=0.78, 212k→50k, +11 F1) from the live slide and deploying it on demand, which both protects later slides' impact and signals confidence the candidate has more evidence than shown.

## Implications

- The "measurement, not preference" hero line is structurally important beyond slide 9 — it's a binary contrast that protects every iteration-based decision elsewhere in the talk. Any time a panellist asks "why this choice?", the candidate can fall back to "the measured signal said X" rather than "I judged that Y".
- The reserved-numbers technique is now in the candidate's toolkit. Slides 11 (κ=0.78 lands here with full context), 12 (the corpus diversity discussion), and 18 (the +11 F1 ablation) will all benefit because slide 9 has primed but not exhausted their numbers.
- The rhetorical pattern from earlier slides — operational framing over technological framing, external signals over internal feelings — now extends to methodology: empirical iteration over preferential iteration. The talk has a consistent posture: external referents trump internal mental states.
- The "is this just engineering?" attack now has a prepared answer with a sharp contrast ("engineering ships; design science iterates with measured signals"). This is the canonical defence for an applied thesis defending its research status.

## What this does not yet establish

- The candidate has not been live-drilled on resisting the urge to use "I felt / I thought" language during Beat 2. The lesson includes the do/don't framing; muscle-memory test is future work.
- The candidate has not practised deploying the reserved numbers in real time when probed mid-defense — only the qualitative live delivery has been scripted. A future drill should rehearse "panellist interrupts with 'how do you know that loop worked?' on each of the three loops" with the right reserved number deployed within 5 seconds.
- The Hevner-Peffers references are included for purists but the candidate hasn't been quizzed on the design-science guidelines themselves. If a methodology specialist sits on the panel, that's the next defence layer to build.
