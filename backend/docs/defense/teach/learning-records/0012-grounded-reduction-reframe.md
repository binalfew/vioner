# The grounded-reduction reframe + the recovery story as architecture

Slide 10 introduces a craft skill that will transfer to every contribution slide that follows: the *grounded-reduction reframe*. When a contribution looks smaller than what the proposal promised (26 entities → 8), the candidate must refuse to apologise for the reduction and instead recast it as a *strengthening of the remaining signal* — every retained label has verifiable ground truth — while the dropped types are *recovered downstream* by an architectural component (KB lookup, taxonomy classifier). The reframe lives in three load-bearing words: *measure → route → decompose*.

## Implications

- The reframe is structurally important beyond slide 10. Slide 11 (corpus: 212k → 50k) faces the same shape of attack — "you used less data, isn't that weakening?" — and the same reframe applies: *the diversity sample preserves rare-class coverage that the raw extract drowned*. Slide 12 (KB) faces "you hard-coded knowledge, isn't that brittle?" — same shape: *the KB is a high-precision pathway for entities the model can't ground from text alone*. The reframe is reusable across slides 10–12.
- The recovery story — naming where each dropped entity goes architecturally — is what makes the reframe land. Without the architectural routing (*"EVENT_TYPE → taxonomy classifier; COUNTRY → KB lookup"*), "we dropped 18 entities" still sounds like loss. With it, "dropped" reads as *"routed to a higher-precision pathway"*. The architecture itself is the reframe's evidence.
- The rhetorical pattern from earlier slides extends to contributions: *external-evidence words over internal-feeling words* (slide 9), *operational framing over technological framing* (slide 7), and now *decomposition over deletion* (slide 10). The deck's consistent posture — every reduction is a routing, every choice is a measured signal, every contribution is an architectural decomposition — runs through the entire contribution arc.
- The "why eighty percent?" attack now has a prepared answer with two layers: (1) the *elbow* argument grounds the threshold in the data; (2) *"design parameter, not discovered constant"* makes the methodology robust to disagreement with the specific number. The same two-layer defence will apply to any threshold the candidate had to set (κ cutoffs, F1 reporting thresholds, training-set size).
- The MOTIVE concession is a controlled honesty move: by naming the limitation cleanly and routing it to future work, the candidate inoculates against the "you skipped the most interesting category" attack. The honest concession preserves credibility for the eight entities that *are* claimed.

## What this does not yet establish

- The candidate has not been live-drilled on the "apology" test — running Beat 3 (table walk) without using "unfortunately", "only", "just", "had to", "couldn't". The lesson includes the do/don't framing; muscle-memory drill is future work.
- The candidate has not practised the per-row drill: what is the recovery story for ORGANIZATION? for INJURED? Some recoveries are stronger than others. ORGANIZATION → KB lookup is robust (the 150-armed-group list is well-curated); INJURED / DISPLACEMENT / DAMAGE → currently *not* recovered, only conceded. A future drill should rehearse fielding "what about INJURED — surely casualty figures matter?" with the honest non-recovery answer.
- The candidate has not been quizzed on the *protocol* of the grounding pilot itself — sample size, annotator count, inter-annotator agreement *on the grounding decision*. If a methodology specialist sits on the panel, the pilot's construction will be probed; the canonical answer ("hand-annotated sample of X articles by Y annotators; verbatim-locatable / not-verbatim-locatable was a binary judgement with κ on that decision of Z") is the next defence layer to build.
- The "design parameter not discovered constant" line is powerful but untested under pressure. A drill where the panellist counters with "you're hiding behind methodology language" would teach the candidate to deploy the elbow + replication argument concretely rather than retreating to vocabulary.
