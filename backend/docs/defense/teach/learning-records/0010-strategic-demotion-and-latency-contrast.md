# Strategic demotion of the model + latency contrast delivery

Slide 8 is structurally unusual — it introduces VioNER's five components but *deliberately demotes the model* to "one component". Most defence candidates instinctively highlight the model as the headline contribution; the script does the opposite. The reason is the integration thread from slide 2: if the panel walks away thinking "this person fine-tuned BERT for African NER" then the four-part contribution claim collapses to a single component, and the thesis loses its design-science framing.

## Implications

- Strategic demotion is its own delivery skill. The candidate has to say "the model is one component" with conviction, not apology — which requires understanding *why* demotion strengthens the contribution claim.
- The latency contrast (150 ms vs 15-25 min) is a separate craft: dramatic juxtaposition of two numbers in different units, without over-narrating the ratio. The contrast speaks for itself.
- The "checkpoint on a laptop" → "capability an analyst can drive" image is the operational version of the integration thread. It transforms abstract integration into a concrete before/after picture.

## What this does not yet establish

- Whether the candidate can hold the demotion line under hostile pressure ("but isn't the model the actual contribution?"). The lesson includes the prepared answer; live drill is future work.
