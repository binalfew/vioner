Strategic demotion — naive vs savvy
Naive (most candidates)

"The core is the fine-tuned BERT. Schema, KB, taxonomy, platform support it." → Panel hears: "you fine-tuned BERT."

Savvy (this script)

"The model is one component." → Panel hears: "integrated system, evaluated end-to-end."

The "no emphasis" rule

Do NOT vocally emphasise "fine-tuned BERT NER model" in the five-component list. Your voice naturally wants to — you worked harder on it than the others. Resist. Same weight as "curated knowledge base". Record yourself and check.
