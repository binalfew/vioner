Before you speak — first-5-seconds protocol
  1. Anchor feet. Shoulder-width. No pacing.
  2. One breath. 3 sec in, 3 sec out.
  3. Eye contact with chair. One second of direct contact.
  4. Now speak. Start Beat 1.
Beat 1 — greet and name the work (15 s)
Say slowly. First impression beats everything else. "Good morning, distinguished examiners. I'm Binalfew Kassa. The thesis I'm presenting today is VioNER — Violent-event Named Entity Recognition. It's an end-to-end, deployable system that converts English-language news about violent events in Africa into structured, queryable 5W1H records."
Beat 2 — roadmap and plant the headline (15 s)
"Plant early" is the strategic verb. Land 0.909 calmly. "In the next thirty minutes I'll cover the problem, the four-part contribution, the evidence, and the limits I own honestly. The headline result I want to plant early: micro F1 of 0.909 on held-out data, with rare-entity recovery that actually saves analyst time."
The number to plant on slide 1

Beat 3 — the three-part frame (15 s)
End on "end-to-end". Clean stop. Walk to slide 2. "The frame for the whole talk: a real operational bottleneck, methodologically open in the literature, closed by an integrated artefact I built and evaluated end-to-end."
1. Real operational bottleneck — AU-CEWS, ACLED, UNHCR · 15-25 min/article
2. Methodologically open — four operational gaps unclosed by existing systems
3. Closed by integrated artefact — five contributions, evaluated end-to-end with UAT
Body language — four techniques
