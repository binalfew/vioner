The 60-second pitch
"The ablation in §6.6 is the empirical defence of the loss contribution. Four configurations, identical conditions, only the loss varies. VICTIM reads: 0.708, 0.776, 0.792, 0.817. Plus eleven on VICTIM, plus 7.2 on ACTION, no common-class regression. Three seeds, variance ±0.4, so gain exceeds variance 25×. Paired bootstrap p < 0.01. This is what makes the loss choice a defensible contribution rather than an assertion."
Three attacks, three answers
1
How do you know this isn't a lucky run?
3 seeds; variance ±0.4 F1; +11 lift exceeds variance by 25×; paired bootstrap p < 0.01.
2
Why these four configurations?
Isolates the design space of the loss contribution. γ value, weight cap, label smoothing → sensitivity analysis in §5.5 (different question, different table).
3
Could a third ingredient have beaten this?
Possibly. Label smoothing tested in Table 6.4 (calibration gain, marginal F1). Dice loss and effective-number weighting → future work item 4. Claim is bounded: combination beats baseline, not globally optimal.
