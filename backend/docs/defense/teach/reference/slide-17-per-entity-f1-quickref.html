Pre-loaded interrupts

Q · "Why is VICTIM weakest — isn't it what matters most?"
Two causes: 5,492 spans vs 47,612 ACTOR (9× less signal) + extreme phrasing variability ("civilians" → "the bus driver's family"). Judo turn: because it matters most, the recipe targets it — next slide shows +11 F1 over plain CE. 0.817 IS the lifted number. Remainder = structural (lim. 2, FW 4).
Q · "P > R everywhere — systematically missing entities?"
Calibrated to miss rather than guess. Mechanism: uncertain ⇒ O. Direction: right for review — false alarm = deletion + eroded trust in every chip; miss = one addition. Knob: per-category thresholds tunable (§4.7). Designed trade, stated openly.
Q · "DISTRICT has 4× VICTIM's data, same F1 — contradiction?"
No — two failure modes. VICTIM = scarcity + variability. DISTRICT = ambiguity with plenty of data: districts share names with cities/regions (Goma = city + district + regional centre). Model defaults to CITY (more often right). More data can't fix ambiguous surface forms. Confusion matrix on slide 20.
Q · "Are these per-entity differences significant?"
Seed variance ±0.4 pp; rare rows wider — conceded. But the structure is robust: 14-pt spread, tier gaps 3–5 pts, rankings stable across 3 seeds. Not defended: REGION .891 vs CASUALTIES .885 — within noise, no conclusion drawn from it. Tiers built on robust gaps only.

Delivery traps to avoid

  • Reciting the ablation progression here (0.708 → 0.776 → 0.792 → 0.817) ⇒ steals slide 18's punchline. Endpoints only, if forced.
  • Reading the ranking without the operational tags ⇒ the slide becomes "more numbers after slide 16". The instructions ARE the slide.
  • Glossing P>R as coincidence ⇒ it's a designed trade; read the direction out loud like slide 16's micro/macro gap.
  • Defending one-point differences (REGION vs CASUALTIES) ⇒ within seed noise. Name it as undefended — honesty deposit.
  • Improvising new excuses (annotation quality, span length) ⇒ contradicts κ = 0.78 story; stick to the documented causes.
  • Rushing Beat 3 ⇒ the five instructions are the hero and the panel's residue. The recital is for fluency; the instructions are for memory.
