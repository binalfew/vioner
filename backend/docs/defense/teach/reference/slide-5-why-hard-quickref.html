Slide 5 — Why this is hard (4 challenges)

Opener: "Why automating this is harder than generic NER. Four things." (half-second pause)
Four challenges — verbatim
One · domain entities · 17 s "One — domain entities. Generic models tag Al-Shabaab as a generic ORGANIZATION, don't know JNIM or RSF, and the African armed-group landscape is dynamic — groups splinter, rebrand, recombine."
Two · class imbalance · 17 s · SLOW THE 78% "Two — severe class imbalance. Seventy-eight percent of tokens are non-entity O, and the operationally critical entities — victim, action, casualties — are the rarest, just a few percent each."
Three · geographic ambiguity · 15 s · LIFT ON GOMA "Three — geographic ambiguity. 'Fighting in Goma' could be city, district, or region. This is why DISTRICT is the weakest entity in the final results."
Four · 5W1H + multi-event · 18 s · PAUSE BEFORE "AND" "Four — 5W1H grouping with perpetrator-versus-victim distinction is not standard NER output. (pause) And multi-event articles pack several incidents into one piece, which means the model has to segment as well as extract."

Internal shape per item (vary your cadence)
OneTopic + 3 named examples + a triplet ("splinter, rebrand, recombine"). Steady; names get small emphasis.
TwoTopic + statistic (78%) + 3 rarest entities. SLOW on the statistic — it's the heaviest fact.
ThreeTopic + quote example + consequence. LIFT on the quote, drop on consequence.
FourTopic + second sub-point with "And". PAUSE before "And"; this is the longest item.
