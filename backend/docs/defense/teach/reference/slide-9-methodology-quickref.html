Three pre-loaded interrupts

"Is this just engineering?"
"Engineering makes implementation choices to ship. Design science iterates on a research artefact with a measured signal closing each loop. Each loop on screen closed on a specific signal — schema grounding score, rare-class F1, the §6.6 ablation. The §6.6 ablation is the embedded controlled experiment within the larger design-science programme."
"Why design science instead of a controlled experiment?"
"Controlled experiments fit single-technique claims — one variable, vary, measure. VioNER's claim is an integrated artefact evaluated across multiple criteria. Design science is the established frame for that. The §6.6 ablation is the embedded controlled experiment within the larger programme."
"What if a loop had failed?"
"Each loop's exit criterion was empirical, not preference-based. If the schema loop hadn't produced higher agreement, I'd have kept iterating until it did, or documented why convergence wasn't possible. The exit criterion is the criterion; failure modes are visible in the iteration record."
