"Is this just ACLED with different labels?"
"No, two reasons. Synthesis: backbone from ACLED, State branch from UCDP, Political L2 from PMVE. Extension: pastoralist–farmer and cattle raiding aren't in any source at this granularity. ACLED's nearest bucket misclassifies the bilateral resource-competition nature."
"What's at Level 3 under [family]?"
"About 25 under Political (or ~30 under Communal, etc.) — full list in Annex B and backup slide B5 if you'd like to look now."
"How did you validate ninety-five is the right granularity?"
"Bottom-up construction — 200 sampled ACLED events assigned to the most specific category that fit. Independent second-annotator kappa at L3 was 0.76. Annex B has the leaves and the protocol."
