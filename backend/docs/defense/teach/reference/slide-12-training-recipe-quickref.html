The production formula

CE   ×   wc   ×   (1 − py)γ Cross-entropy × class weight × focusing modulator

Ingredient 1

Class weights

Across CLASSES

One weight per class. Rare classes get a large weight; common ones small.

wc ∝ 1 / freq(c), capped at 10

Without it: model learns "get O right almost always".

Ingredient 2

Focal loss (γ = 2)

Across DIFFICULTY

Per-token modulator. Easy-correct → near zero; hard → near one.

(1 − py)²   — Lin et al. 2017

Without it: model polishes tokens it already gets right.

130×

A VICTIM token contributes ~130× the loss-weight of an O token under the production recipe. That counter-balances the 78 % O dominance.

The three-move complementarity defence

1Name the two ingredients. "Inverse-frequency class weights and focal loss." Technical names — panel knows them.
2Name the two distinct axes. "Across classes" vs "across difficulty." ← without distinct axes, the defence collapses into double-counting.
3Point to the ablation as evidence. "Slide 18 / §6.6 — neither alone reaches the combined performance."

Four pre-loaded interrupts

"Why both? Isn't it double-counting?"
"No — different aspects of imbalance. Class weights across classes (VICTIM ≈ 130× O). Focal loss across difficulty within a class — (1−p)² shrinks easy-correct tokens. Class weights alone still let the model polish easy VICTIMs; focal alone can't lift the per-class signal when VICTIM is rare. §6.6 ablation shows neither alone reaches combined."
"Why γ = 2?"
"Lin et al. 2017 default. Tested γ=1 (smaller gain — spotlight not sharp enough) and γ=3 (training instability — moderately confident tokens also fade). γ=2 empirically stable for our O dominance."
"Why cap at 10?"
"Empirical. Unclipped VICTIM and CASUALTIES weights hit ~22 and destabilise training in the first few hundred steps. 10 keeps rare classes elevated without gradient blow-up. Search documented in §5.5."
"Did you try dice / contrastive / only label smoothing?"
"Label smoothing β=0.1 IS in the production loss — marginal effect. Dice is a fair future comparison, not tested — focal loss has more direct prior work in token classification under severe imbalance. Contrastive is out of scope — different training regime."
