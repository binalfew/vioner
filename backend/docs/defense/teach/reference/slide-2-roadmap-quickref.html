Beat 1 — the arc (20 s)
Say in one breath "This is the roadmap. I move from why the problem matters, to why existing tools don't close it, to the four contributions, then the evidence — model metrics, the ablation, KB impact, and user testing — and finally the limits and deployment path."
— half-second pause — eye contact with one panellist —
Beat 3 — the integration thread (20 s)
Say slower than Beat 1 "One thread ties the whole talk together: each contribution closes one operational gap, and dropping any single piece breaks the chain. The contribution is the integration, not any one component."
The hero sentence — memorise verbatim "The contribution is the integration, not any one component."
What this one sentence pre-empts

Five forbidden moves
Don't read the listPanel can read. Name the shape.
Don't repeat "I'll talk about"Use one verb for the whole arc.
Don't trail offEnd on "component". Walk to slide 3.
Don't apologise"It's a lot…" cuts your authority.
Don't go past 60 sAt 60 s panel zones out. At 90 s they're forming questions.
If interrupted mid-Beat-1

Finish the current clause. Say: "Happy to take that — and I'll come back to the rest of the roadmap after." Answer briefly. Then return to deliver Beat 3 — the integration thread is non-negotiable, regardless of what got interrupted.
