The 100-second script

1
~15 s
"RQ1 — the schema. The original proposal called for twenty-six entity types. Before committing the training compute, I needed to know which could actually be supervised."
2
~30 s
"In November 2025, I ran a grounding pilot. A sample of articles was hand-annotated; for each entity type I measured the fraction that could be located verbatim in source text — a phrase the analyst could point at, not a category they had to infer. Types below eighty percent grounding were dropped from the supervised schema."
3
~35 s
"What remained were eight entities — ACTOR, VICTIM, ACTION, DATE, REGION, CITY, DISTRICT, CASUALTIES. In BIO encoding, seventeen labels. The dropped types are not lost — EVENT_TYPE is reconstructed by the taxonomy classifier; COUNTRY by a KB lookup. Eight clean training signals plus two free downstream entities."
4
~20 s
"That trade — fewer entities, but every one with a verifiable ground-truth signal — was the single most consequential methodological choice of the thesis. The schema didn't get smaller as a concession. It got trustworthy."

Do / Don't on stage

Do say

  • "The pilot measured grounding."
  • "Routed below-threshold types elsewhere."
  • "Schema is decomposed, not narrower."
  • "Every retained label has verifiable ground truth."
  • "Recovered downstream by KB / taxonomy."
  • "Eighty was the elbow of the distribution."

Don't say

  • "Unfortunately..."
  • "We had to drop..."
  • "Eighteen entities didn't work."
  • "Too hard / too noisy."
  • "Eighty felt reasonable."
  • "Best for our metrics."

Three pre-loaded interrupts

"Isn't reducing 26 → 8 a weakening?"
"It's a strengthening. Types below eighty-percent grounding are routed to higher-precision pathways — EVENT_TYPE to the taxonomy classifier, COUNTRY to the KB. Every retained label has verifiable ground truth. The schema isn't narrower; it's decomposed."
"How did you pick the eighty-percent threshold?"
"The elbow of the grounding-rate distribution — types clustered above eighty or below sixty, with little in between. Reported as a design parameter, not a discovered constant; if a future replication finds the elbow elsewhere, the methodology is unchanged."
"What about MOTIVE?"
"MOTIVE was the worst offender — almost never written verbatim; analysts infer it from prior knowledge of the actor. Noted as future work — the next layer of the architecture, not this layer. Causal inference on top of extraction."
